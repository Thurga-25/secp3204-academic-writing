## Abstract

Tools that use Generative AI (GenAI), such as GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT, are increasingly being used in software engineering education for code writing and debugging tasks. However, the impact of these tools on learning among data engineering students is not clear, and the number of studies dedicated to this subgroup is limited. The present work is a literature review of existing studies about the impact of GenAI on students’ learning in data engineering and related computing fields. The relevant literature was found through academic databases using keywords related to GenAI, code writing, debugging and software engineering education. The results reveal that GenAI makes it possible to receive quick feedback and explanations provided about coding tasks, but they also raise important concerns about the reliance on such technology, incorrect information provided, and a cloud of authorship around the material produced. It is recommended that students should always double check the results generated using AI technologies, and make sure that they depend on their own thinking rather than allowing artificial intelligence (AI) to make decisions for them. Educators need to focus on the idea of AI literacy and consider its teaching in their classes.

## Introduction and Problem Statement

Applications of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) such as ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot are becoming popular in computer programming education for the purpose of code writing, error correction or clarification (IJACSA authors, 2026; Strik, Menolli and Brancher, 2024). For instance, students enrolled in software engineering programs can describe a certain task and get a working code almost instantly or simply write something wrong and obtain a fix right away. This is very important for the education of data engineers, as high quality of pipelines hinges on development and implementation and life cycle management techniques as much as on data processing skills (Foidl et al., 2023). However, acquiring these techniques requires plenty of effort that makes the temptation to get quick AI answers even higher among students.

Previous research has indicated that GenAI can help improve effectiveness and aid creativity. However, there are certain issues raised about the occurrence of erroneous output, propagation of mistakes and unclear authorship (Reihanian et al., 2024). For example, while debugging, beginner programmers often place excessive dependence upon the model,simply following its educational advice (Bo et al., 2025); furthermore, solutions that give total fixes may adversely affect independent debugging capabilities (Kurniawan et al., 2025). In addition, most evidence comes from introductory programming courses and computing, while research of the data-focused users is just beginning (Bien and Mukherjee, 2025).

Thus, the difficulty lies in the fact that there is no properly substantiated evidence of how GenAI helps or disrupts software engineering learning in the context of code generation and debugging among data engineering students, which complicates providing any recommendations about its usage. The paper aims to fill the existing void in the field by reviewing the current literature on how GenAI assists or disrupts the development of software engineering knowledge in terms of code generation and debugging for data engineering learners.

## Research Objectives

- RO1: Identify how GenAI tools are utilized for creating programs and fixing bugs in software engineering education among data engineering and related information technology students.<br>
- RO2: Examine advantages and disadvantages of these applications mentioned in research publications.<br>
- RO3: Propose ways for learners and educators to apply GenAI safely in the field of software engineering education.


## Research Questions

- RQ1: What are the methods for applying GenAI in coding and debugging in students from fields concerning data engineering and computation?<br>
- RQ2: What are the possible advantages and disadvantages of the mentioned approaches according to studies?<br>
- RQ3: Which practices allow the use of GenAI in an efficient and safe way within the realm of software engineering learning?
